About the Forum

Empowering future leaders through continuous skill enhancement, industry alignment, and collaborative opportunities.

Vision

“ To be recognized as a center of excellence in the field of information Technology where inquisitive minds are fostered, leading to skill professionals for satisfying the need of society. ”

Mission

"Apply knowledge of engineering fundamentals and cutting-edge technology to identify and implements innovative solutions for engineering problems and issues in society."

"Build strong interpersonal skill and will engage in life-long learning to enhance their career positions, both as team members and leaders.”

Pragyan serves as the collective student-faculty node for the Department of Computer Science & Engineering (Data Science). Our core motivation is to empower the next generation of engineers, builders, and data thinkers to exceed standard academic bounds.

Through coding hackathons, guest seminars, creative visual workshops, and team dynamics, we construct an environment focused on industry readiness, technical versatility, and proactive leadership. Whether managing state-of-the-art database systems, designing graphical modules, or organizing inter-college sports trials, Pragyan members drive practical growth.
